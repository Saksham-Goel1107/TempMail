Privacy Policy

At TempMail Pro, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our temporary email service.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use our service. Our service is designed to be completely anonymous.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use our service, we may automatically collect certain information:

  • IP address (temporarily stored for security purposes)
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Usage patterns and timestamps

1.3 Email Content

We temporarily store emails sent to your temporary address. However:

  • Email content is automatically deleted after a short period
  • We do not read, analyze, or share email content
  • Emails are stored securely and encrypted

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Providing the temporary email service
  • Ensuring service security and preventing abuse
  • Improving service performance and reliability
  • Complying with legal obligations

3. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your information:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure data storage with encryption at rest
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access to data on a need-to-know basis

4. Data Retention

Our data retention policy is designed to minimize data storage:

  • Temporary email addresses: Automatically deleted after expiration
  • Email content: Automatically deleted within 24-48 hours
  • Log data: Retained for maximum 30 days for security purposes
  • No long-term storage of personal information

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights and prevent fraud
  • In connection with a business transfer (with notice)

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your theme preference
  • Provide basic functionality
  • Analyze service usage (with your consent)
