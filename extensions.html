🎯 What is TempMail Pro?

TempMail Pro is the ultimate privacy-focused solution for managing temporary and disposable email addresses directly from your browser. It offers a seamless, secure, and efficient way to keep your real inbox free from spam, while maintaining complete anonymity online.

⚡ Lightning Fast & Real-time

Generate a temporary email instantly with just one click. View incoming messages in real-time right within your browser—no need to switch tabs. The autofill email feature makes it effortless to get the email address filled and use it wherever you need.

🛡️ Privacy First, Always

There's absolutely no registration or setup required. Simply open TempMail Pro, and you're ready to go. Every session is private and secure, ensuring your personal identity remains completely protected from unwanted advertisers or spam.

🎨 Perfect for Every Use Case

Perfect for quick website sign-ups, online testing, temporary registrations, and keeping your main inbox clean. Whether you're a developer testing email functions, a privacy-conscious user, or just someone tired of spam—TempMail Pro makes your online experience safer and more efficient.

🚀 Download & Install

Follow these simple steps to install the TempMail extension on your Microsoft Edge browser:

  1. Open Microsoft Edge: Launch your Microsoft Edge browser on your computer.
  2. Visit the Microsoft Edge Add-ons Store: Go to the official Microsoft Edge Add-ons website or click the download button below.
  3. Find TempMail Pro Disposable: Search for "TempMail Pro Disposable" in the store, or use the direct link provided.
  4. Install the Extension: Click the "Get" button to add the extension to your browser.

🎯 How to Use

Once installed, using TempMail Pro is incredibly simple. Here's how to get started:

1

Click the Extension Icon

Look for the TempMail Pro icon in your browser toolbar and click it to open the extension popup.

2

Generate Email

Click the "Generate New Email" button to instantly create a fresh temporary email address.

3

Copy & Use

Copy the generated email address and use it wherever you need a temporary email.

4

Check Messages

Return to the extension to view any incoming messages in real-time.

5

Auto-fill

On websites, the extension will show a button to automatically fill email fields for you.
